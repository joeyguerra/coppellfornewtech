# New Tech Parent Group Charter School Plan

## 1. High-Level Concept
Form a **for-profit or nonprofit charter school management LLC** backed by the New Tech parent group.

The LLC would:
- **Apply for an in-district charter** (or potentially an open-enrollment charter) to operate a high school using the New Tech model.
- **Bid for the current New Tech facility** when CISD declares it surplus or negotiable.
- **Contract with AI as an “Operator Partner”** to handle educational innovation, operational efficiency, compliance documentation, and grant/funding applications.

## 2. Structure & Roles

| Role | Entity / Person | Responsibilities |
|------|----------------|------------------|
| **Founding Members** | Core parent group | Form the LLC, initial governance, fundraising |
| **AI Operator Partner** | AI advisory role | Operational intelligence, compliance documents, grant writing, marketing, curriculum integration |
| **Education Director** | Certified administrator | Academic leadership, staff hiring, TEA compliance |
| **Finance & Ops Lead** | Parent or hired professional | Budgeting, vendor contracts, facilities, legal |
| **Board of Managers** | 5–7 people | Strategic oversight |

## 3. Legal Pathway

### Step 1: Form the LLC
- File with the Texas Secretary of State.
- Decide if for-profit or nonprofit (nonprofit is advantageous for grants and perception).
- Draft an **Operating Agreement** for governance, roles, and finances.

### Step 2: Charter Application
Two main options:

1. **In-District Charter (Texas Ed. Code § 12.051)**  
   - Partner with CISD.  
   - Requires school board approval.  
   - Easier building negotiations.

2. **Open-Enrollment Charter**  
   - Approved by the Texas Education Agency (TEA).  
   - Operates independently.  
   - Requires separate lease/purchase for the building.

### Step 3: Facility Acquisition
- Monitor CISD for **Declaration of Surplus Property** on New Tech.
- Submit offer to purchase or lease.
- Include community benefit commitments.

## 4. Funding Strategy

**Startup Capital Sources:**
- Founding member contributions
- Community investment fund
- Federal Charter School Program (CSP) grants
- Philanthropic foundations (Walton Family Foundation, Gates Foundation)
- Naming rights donors

**Ongoing Revenue:**
- Per-pupil state funding (~$7,000/student in Texas)
- STEM/PBL program grants
- Community facility use rentals

## 5. Academic & Operational Plan
- Keep **project-based learning** as brand identity.
- Maintain **small-school feel**.
- Offer **dual credit** and **industry certifications**.
- **AI Support**:
  - Curriculum mapping & TEKS alignment
  - Data dashboards for progress & compliance
  - Grant writing automation
  - Marketing & enrollment campaigns

## 6. Marketing & Enrollment

**Brand Launch:**  
- Possible name: *New Tech Academy*.

**Target Audience:**  
- Students seeking a smaller, innovative school  
- Out-of-district families willing to commute  

**Recruitment Campaign:**  
- Website & social media  
- Feeder middle school visits  
- Community open houses  
- Alumni success stories  

**Enrollment Goal:**  
- Year 1: 200–250 students (break-even)  
- Year 3: 350–400 students (capacity)

## 7. Timeline

| Phase | Actions | Timeframe |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| **Organizing** | Form LLC, recruit board, legal counsel | 1–2 months |
| **Application** | Charter application, CISD building talks | 4–6 months |
| **Funding & Facility** | Grants, investors, facility deal | Parallel to application |
| **Pre-Launch** | Hire staff, marketing, enrollment | 6–9 months before opening |
| **Launch** | Open with AI operational support | Year 1 |
| **Scale** | Expand programs & enrollment | Years 2–5 |

## 8. Risks & Mitigation

- **Risk:** CISD refuses to sell/lease.  
  *Mitigation:* Have backup facilities.
- **Risk:** Charter application denied.  
  *Mitigation:* Operate as private school, reapply.
- **Risk:** Low enrollment.  
  *Mitigation:* Aggressive marketing, expand beyond CISD.
