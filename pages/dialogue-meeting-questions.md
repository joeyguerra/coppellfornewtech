# CISD Dialogue Meetings

CISD is hosting Dialogue Meetings to better understand what our community values in order to feed into their design framework for deciding on which schools to close.

## Meeting Schedule:

| School | Date | Time | Location |
| -------| -----| -----| ---------|
| Lakeside Elementary | August 14 | 5:30–6:30 PM | Cafeteria |
| Cottonwood Creek Elementary | August 18 | 5:30–6:30 PM | Cafeteria |
| Mockingbird Elementary | August 20 | 5:30–6:30 PM | Cafeteria |
| Austin Elementary | August 21 | 5:30–6:30 PM | Cafeteria |
| Town Center Elementary | August 28 | 5:30–6:30 PM | Cafeteria |

## Survey Questions for Breakout Groups

1. What questions or concerns do you have about the budget information shared?
1. What should CISD consider in the elementary planning process? What factors should CISD look at and consider?
1. What guiding principles would ensure planning reflects our community's needs and strengths?
1. If you were to reimagine elementary school, what innovations would you include?
1. What do families and staff need from the district to feel informed and supported?
1. Share any feedback or questions.
